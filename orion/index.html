Origin About me

I read data the way astronomers read light.

I'm Mohit Rai, a developer from India and a 2nd-year student of the BS in Data Science & Programming at IIT Madras.

I work across two disciplines that usually live apart: building full-stack products, and analysing the data that flows through them. I'm happiest when a project needs both a clean interface and an honest look at the numbers behind it.

Astronomers learn almost everything about a star from its light: its colour, its spectrum, the way it shifts. I try to treat data the same way. One day I'd like a career where astronomy and technology meet.
